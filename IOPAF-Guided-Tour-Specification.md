# IOPAF Guided Capability Tour

The tour introduces the page rather than duplicating it. It uses five short steps: **one lifecycle**, **evidence-gated maturity**, **target-scoped IAM**, **audited Bayesian risk**, and **decision outputs**. Each step identifies the management question, explains the IOPAF response in two sentences, and points to the existing visual chapter.

The hero carries one “Take the guided tour” action. Selecting it opens a fixed technical briefing panel with step progress, previous, next, skip and finish controls. The corresponding chapter scrolls into view and receives a restrained teal highlight rule. Content remains readable without the tour.

Keyboard behavior is explicit: focus enters the tour panel on open; Left and Right Arrow move between steps; Escape closes; Tab remains within the visible controls; closing restores focus to the launch button. Mobile uses a bottom briefing sheet with the same content and controls. Reduced-motion users receive immediate scrolling and no entrance transition.
